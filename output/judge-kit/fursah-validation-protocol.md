# Controlled pilot validation protocol

## Objective

Test whether Fursah improves the quality and speed of evidence-based readiness decisions without creating unacceptable privacy, fairness, accessibility, or automation risks.

## Minimum design

- 8–12 weeks with at least one university program and one employer partner.
- A comparison baseline using the institution’s current advising workflow.
- Synthetic data in rehearsal; consented, minimized participant data in the controlled pilot.
- Pre-registered success metrics and stop conditions.

## Primary measures

- Time from initial profile to verified readiness plan.
- Percentage of recommended actions completed.
- Evidence completion and rejection rates.
- Agreement between system recommendations and blinded human reviewers.
- Appeal, override, and correction rates.

## Safety measures

- Outcome parity across approved comparison groups.
- Low-confidence recommendation rate.
- Cross-role access-test failures.
- Accessibility task-completion rate.
- Stale-source and missing-provenance rate.

## Governance gates

1. Privacy and security review before participant onboarding.
2. Human approval of all extracted evidence.
3. Weekly incident and appeal review.
4. Mid-pilot fairness and accessibility checkpoint.
5. Final external review before any scale decision.

## Stop conditions

Pause the pilot for confirmed cross-role disclosure, systematic unsupported recommendations, unresolved material accessibility barriers, or a statistically and operationally meaningful outcome disparity.

## Success threshold

Proceed only if the pilot improves at least one primary operational outcome, does not materially degrade another, and passes the safety and governance gates. Publish null or negative findings as part of the evidence record.
