# Judge Q&A

## What is actually AI here?

The assistant explains stored readiness evidence, and document extraction proposes structured fields for human review. The readiness score itself is deterministic, published, and versioned.

## How do you prevent hallucinations?

The assistant receives role-scoped platform facts and explicit boundaries. It may explain those facts but cannot invent evidence, calculate scores, verify achievements, or expose another role’s data. The UI discloses model and grounding versions.

## Why use Y.3172?

It provides an end-to-end pipeline structure. Fursah maps all seven clause 8.1 nodes—from source through action—to concrete components and evidence, making the system easier to audit.

## What is your knowledge-base contribution?

Seventeen authentic sources are converted into implementation consequences and exposed through the interface plus versioned JSON and CSV exports. That makes the contribution reusable outside the prototype.

## How does this influence policy?

The prototype turns six observed gaps into operational recommendations with accountable owners, triggers, metrics, and review cadences. These are decision inputs, not claims that a policy has already been adopted.

## Is the prototype production-ready?

It is deployment-ready as a demonstration and includes governance controls, but validated real-world impact requires a controlled institutional pilot, external review, and production-risk acceptance.

## Is the data real?

The demo accounts use synthetic data. External sources in the knowledge base are authentic and linked to their publishers.

## What is the defensible innovation?

Fursah connects individual evidence, labor-market requirements, and institutional action through one inspectable vocabulary while separating deterministic decisions from generative explanation.

## What would you measure in a pilot?

Evidence completion, recommendation acceptance, time to verified readiness, prerequisite-gate recovery, source freshness, confidence distribution, accessibility task completion, outcome parity, and appeal/override rates.

## What should judges not count?

Do not count the work-in-progress business-analysis PDF. Judge the running prototype, standards mapping, published knowledge base, policy outputs, and the completed judge submission report.
